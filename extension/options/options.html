What CareerOS will not do

At any setting: no browser fingerprint spoofing, no captcha solving, no proxy or IP rotation, no masking of automated control. Those cross from a terms question into circumventing a service's access controls, and they're also the fastest way to lose a Web Store listing. When a run hits a captcha or a login wall it stops and tells you.

Auto submit only ever applies to employer ATS platforms. On LinkedIn, Indeed, ZipRecruiter, Dice and Monster, whose terms prohibit automated interaction, CareerOS fills the form and stops regardless of this setting. With auto submit off everywhere, CareerOS fills the form and stops. You read it and press the site's own submit. Leaving it off is the safer habit, and some job boards restrict automated submissions in their terms.
